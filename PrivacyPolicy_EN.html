E-BELT PRO Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026 | Effective date: November 16, 2026

E-BELT PRO (the "App") is the companion app used to connect to and control the E-BELT PRO training belt. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what information the App handles and how.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not collect or transmit any personal information. Specifically:

  • The App has no account system. There is no registration or login, and we do not obtain your phone number, email, or any identity information;
  • The App has no server. All data is stored locally on your device only and is never transmitted to any external server;
  • The App contains no advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or tracking components, and does not perform any cross-app or cross-site tracking;
  • The App does not access your contacts, photos, camera, location, or microphone.

2. Information Stored Locally

The following information is stored only on your device so that the App can function:

  • Bluetooth connection data: identifiers and connection state of paired devices, used to establish and maintain the Bluetooth connection to the belt;
  • Training parameters and history: the training modes and intensity settings you configure, and locally generated training records, used for history and progress views;
  • App preferences: language and interface settings.

This data resides in a local database on your device. We do not access its contents and cannot read it remotely. Uninstalling the App permanently deletes all local data.

3. Permissions

  • Bluetooth: the only system permission the App requests. It is used to scan for and connect to the E-BELT PRO training belt and send training commands. Denying this permission will prevent use of the core training features.

4. Third-Party Libraries

The App uses third-party libraries (Realm local database, YYModel JSON conversion, IQKeyboardManager keyboard handling). All are purely local-function libraries that do not collect or transmit any data.

5. Children

The App is intended for adults, is not directed at children, and does not collect any information from anyone (the App does not collect information at all).

6. Your Rights

  • All data is generated locally by you and stored only on your device. You can delete it entirely at any time by uninstalling the App;
  • If you have questions about this policy or the App's data handling, contact us.

7. Changes to This Policy

If future features require this policy to be updated (for example, if networked features are added), we will publish the updated version on this page with a new date. If any networked feature is added, we will clearly inform you and obtain your consent before collecting any data.

8. Contact Us

Operator / data controller: E-BELT PRO
Contact: You can reach us via the “App Support” link on our App Store product page.
